# 1. About this policy

1.1 This policy explains what personal information SAMSAL collects, why we collect it, how we use, share and protect it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to personal information we collect through our website, by email, telephone, WhatsApp and in the course of dealing with customers and suppliers.

1.2 "Personal information" (or "personal data") means information about an identifiable person, such as a name, email address or telephone number. We supply businesses and institutions, so most of the personal information we hold is about people who work for our customers and suppliers.

1.3 We aim to follow the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya and its regulations. [[Adviser to confirm which obligations apply to SAMSAL and that this policy reflects them.]]

# 2. Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to do business with you.

| Where it comes from | What we collect | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Request a Quote form** | Organisation name, contact person, email address, telephone number, the product or service you need, category, specifications, quantity, delivery location, required delivery date, additional instructions, and an optional file you attach (such as a product list or specification) | To prepare and send a quotation and, if you go ahead, arrange supply and delivery |
| **Business enquiry form** | Name, organisation (optional), email address, telephone number and your message | To respond to your enquiry |
| **Email, telephone and WhatsApp** | The contact details and the content of the messages you send us | To respond and to keep a record of what was agreed |
| **Customer and quotation records** | Contact details, enquiry and quotation history, notes made by our staff, follow-up dates, and the documents we issue to you | To manage the relationship, prepare quotations and follow up |
| **Supplier records** | Supplier contact names, business details and documents the supplier gives us | To source goods and manage purchasing |
| **Website technical data** | Your IP address (used briefly to limit spam and abuse) and standard server logs kept by our hosting provider | To keep the website secure and available |
| **Staff accounts** (our own staff only) | Name, work email address, role, sign-in times and an activity log of actions taken in our administration system | To control access and keep a record of changes |

2.2 Please do not send us sensitive personal information, such as health details, identity document numbers or financial account details. We do not need it to prepare a quotation. If you send it anyway, we will delete it where we can.

2.3 We do not knowingly collect personal information from children (see section 12).

# 3. How and why we use your information

3.1 We use personal information to:

- respond to your requests and enquiries;
- prepare, send and follow up quotations;
- arrange sourcing, order confirmation, supply, delivery, invoicing and payment if you become a customer;
- communicate with you about your request or order;
- keep our records, including customer, supplier and accounting records;
- protect the website and our business from spam, fraud and misuse; and
- meet legal, tax, accounting and regulatory obligations.

3.2 We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for marketing unrelated to your request without your consent. We do not make decisions about you using fully automated processing.

# 4. Our lawful grounds

4.1 We rely on one or more of the following, depending on the situation. [[Adviser to confirm the grounds stated and how consent should be recorded.]]

- **Your consent**, when you choose to send us a form, an email or an attachment. You can withdraw consent at any time (see section 9).
- **Taking steps you ask for before a contract, or performing a contract**, such as preparing a quotation or delivering an order.
- **Our legitimate interests** in responding to enquiries, managing our business relationships and keeping the website secure, balanced against your rights.
- **A legal obligation**, such as keeping accounting and tax records.

# 5. Who we share it with

5.1 We share personal information only where it is needed, and only with providers that help us operate the website and our business. These providers act on our instructions, and we use them under the terms they offer. [[Adviser to confirm that adequate data processing terms are in place with each provider.]]

| Provider | What it does for us |
|---|---|
| Vercel | Hosts the website and processes website requests |
| Supabase | Provides the database, file storage and staff sign-in used for our customer and business records |
| Resend | Delivers form submissions (including attachments) to our business email |
| Upstash (if enabled) | Stores IP-based rate-limit counters for a short time to prevent abuse |
| Cloudflare Turnstile (if enabled) | Checks that a form is submitted by a person and not a robot |
| Our email, accounting and office tools | Receive, store and process business correspondence and records |
| Delivery and transport partners | Receive delivery details (name, address, telephone number) only where needed to deliver your order |

5.2 We may also disclose personal information where the law requires it, to a court or competent authority, to professional advisers (such as lawyers and auditors) under duties of confidentiality, or to protect our rights or safety.

5.3 If our business is ever sold or restructured, personal information may be transferred to the new owner on terms that continue to protect it.

# 8. How we protect it

8.1 We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricting access to staff who need it, role-based permissions in our administration system, server-side checks of form input and file types, keeping credentials on the server rather than in the website, and a record of administrative actions. Our form also uses anti-spam checks.

8.2 No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a security incident affects your personal information, we will take steps to contain it and will notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner and the people affected where the law requires. [[Adviser to confirm the notification duties and timelines that apply.]]

# 9. Your rights

9.1 Under Kenyan data protection law you may have rights over your personal information. These can include the right to:

- be informed about how we use it;
- access the personal information we hold about you;
- object to our processing of it;
- have inaccurate or out-of-date information corrected;
- have information deleted, in some circumstances;
- receive a copy of the information you gave us in a usable format; and
- withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on it.

[[Adviser to confirm the exact rights and any limits that apply.]]

9.2 To use any of these rights, contact us at [[privacy email address to be added]]. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We will respond within [[the time the law requires, to be confirmed by the adviser]]. There is normally no fee. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew it.

# 10. Complaints

10.1 If you have a concern about how we handle your information, please contact us first so that we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya (www.odpc.go.ke).
