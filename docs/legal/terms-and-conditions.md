# Part A. Terms of Supply

## 1. Introduction and definitions

1.1 These terms apply to every quotation we give and every contract for the supply of goods (and any related distribution or delivery services) between SAMSAL and a business or institutional customer.

1.2 In these terms: **"Customer"** means the business, organisation or institution that requests a quotation or places an order; **"Goods"** means the products we agree to supply; **"Quotation"** means a written offer from SAMSAL to supply Goods; **"Order"** means the Customer's acceptance of a Quotation; **"Order Confirmation"** means SAMSAL's written confirmation of an Order; **"Contract"** means the contract formed when an Order is confirmed; **"Business Day"** means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Kenya.

1.3 SAMSAL supplies to businesses and institutions. These terms are not drafted for sales to individual consumers. [[Adviser to confirm whether consumer-protection law needs to be addressed if SAMSAL will also sell to individuals.]]

## 2. Application of these terms

2.1 The Contract is made up of the Order Confirmation, any special terms stated in the Quotation, and these terms. If they conflict, the Order Confirmation prevails, then the Quotation, then these terms.

2.2 Any terms the Customer proposes (for example on a purchase order or in a tender document) do not apply unless SAMSAL expressly accepts them in writing. [[Adviser to confirm how this should work where a public body's tender conditions must apply.]]

2.3 Submitting a request for a quotation through our website, by email, by telephone or by message is a request for information. It is not an order and does not oblige either party.

## 3. Quotations

3.1 A Quotation is prepared from the information the Customer gives us. The Customer must make sure that the product descriptions, specifications, quantities, delivery locations and dates it gives us are complete and accurate.

3.2 A Quotation is valid for [[validity period, for example a number of days, to be confirmed]] from its date, unless it says otherwise. After that, SAMSAL may withdraw or change it.

3.3 We may correct a clear error in a Quotation, price list or Order Confirmation by notifying the Customer. If the corrected price is not acceptable, the Customer may cancel the affected part of the Order without charge before the Goods are dispatched.

3.4 Prices and availability depend on supplier and market conditions. A Quotation is not a reservation of stock unless it says so.

## 4. Orders and acceptance

4.1 An Order is made when the Customer accepts a Quotation in writing (including by email or by a purchase order that matches the Quotation). The Contract is formed only when SAMSAL sends an Order Confirmation.

4.2 We may decline an Order, for example because Goods are unavailable, the Customer's credit has not been approved, or the delivery location is outside our delivery area.

4.3 Changes to an Order must be agreed in writing. We may adjust the price and delivery date to reflect an agreed change.

4.4 **Cancellation.** The Customer may cancel a confirmed Order only with our written agreement. Where Goods have already been sourced, ordered from a supplier or specially prepared for the Customer, the Customer must pay our reasonable costs incurred up to the cancellation.
